What is shared?

Screen video only—no system audio, microphone or camera in this release. Anything visible on your chosen screen can be shared, including notifications. Your system asks for permission each time. Protected content may appear black. The receiver can record only if you allow it in this app; this cannot prevent screenshots or external recording.

WebRTC encrypts media in transit. The connection server processes session credentials and negotiation; a configured TURN relay may carry encrypted media. No analytics are built into Still. The server operator and network providers may process operational records.

Keep the receiving app open. Stop sharing here or in your device’s capture controls at any time.
