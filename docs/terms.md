## 2. What the app provides

Still receives supported media links and playback commands from its bundled browser remote or compatible clients over a local Wi-Fi network. It offers on-device playback, a temporary queue, appearance preferences and session controls. Its own Bonjour advertisement identifies its own protocol. It is not a certified Google Cast, AirPlay or Miracast receiver and uses its own WebRTC screen-sharing mode rather than those protocols.

The receiver must remain in the foreground. iOS can suspend networking, interrupt audio, restrict permissions or terminate the app. Backgrounding stops local commands and WebRTC screen reception. Native direct-stream audio may continue if enabled, and native Picture in Picture can continue where iOS permits it. YouTube pauses. These exceptions do not keep the receiver server alive. The current implementation uses IPv4 Wi-Fi pairing. Supported extensions, codecs, formats and controls are described in the bundled compatibility page and may change as Apple or media providers change their platforms.
